# Community Standards & Ethical Highway Safety Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As contributors, maintainers, and researchers on the **SafarGuard** project, we pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free, welcoming, and productive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community dedicated to saving human lives through ethical safety-critical engineering.

---

## Safety-Critical Engineering Principles

Because SafarGuard is an autonomous highway collision prevention and road safety system, contributors are held to high safety and ethical standards:

1. **Safety First**: No algorithm, code change, or interface design shall ever encourage unsafe driving, reckless speeding, or unauthorized vehicle maneuvers.
2. **Deterministic Fail-Safes**: All AI vision features must maintain deterministic mathematical fallbacks (such as the AASHTO stopping sight distance equations) in the event of sensor disconnection, optical degradation, or network outage.
3. **Data Integrity**: Datasets submitted from provincial emergency services (Rescue 1122, NHMP, PDMA) must be verified, unmanipulated, and accurately cited.
4. **Privacy and Non-Surveillance**: Telematics must respect driver privacy. Driver identity, private dashcam streams, and location logs must never be harvested or monetized.

## Major League Hacking (MLH) Code of Conduct Adherence

SafarGuard was created under the auspices of **Hacktoberfest Hack Day Lahore 2026** and upholds the official **Major League Hacking (MLH) Code of Conduct**:
- Hackers must be respectful and courteous to peers, judges, organizers, and the broader open-source community.
- Harassment and abusive behavior are not tolerated in any physical or virtual space.
- Academic and engineering honesty is enforced: all third-party libraries, datasets, and open-source models are openly attributed.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall road safety community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
