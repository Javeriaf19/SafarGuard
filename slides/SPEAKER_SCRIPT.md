## Slide 2: Ground Reality & Datasets (35 Seconds)
**Visual**: Emergency metrics and dynamic Chart.js comparative stopping distance graph.  
**Key Stats**: Rescue 1122 +34.8% surge, friction drop to 0.28.

> *"We examined official Punjab Rescue 1122 emergency dispatch logs: during winter smog months, highway collision dispatches surge by **34.8%**.  
> 
> Why? Because when PM2.5 soot coalesces with overnight cold dew, the road friction coefficient collapses from dry asphalt's 0.70 down to **0.28**.  
> As you can see in this chart, at 100 km/h, braking distance more than doubles from 73 meters to over 160 meters. Vehicles literally outdrive what their drivers can see."*

## Slide 3: The Science & Google Gemma 4 (40 Seconds)
**Visual**: AASHTO formulas ($SSD$ & $CPI$) and Google DeepMind Gemma 4 Vision card.

> *"We solved this with deterministic physics and Google DeepMind's Gemma 4.  
> 
> First, our engine calculates **AASHTO Stopping Sight Distance**:  
> If Stopping Distance exceeds Visibility, the Crash Probability Index (CPI) exceeds 1.0, meaning a pileup is mathematically guaranteed unless speed is capped immediately.  
> 
> Second, we deploy **Gemma 4 Multimodal Vision**: it audits forward dashcam frames in real-time to detect blinding high-beam backscatter and low-contrast obstacles like unlit sugarcane tractor-trolleys."*

## Slide 4: The 5-Interface System Architecture (30 Seconds)
**Visual**: Overview of all 5 interfaces designed for the complete highway ecosystem.

> *"Safety requires reaching all stakeholders. We designed a unified ecosystem:  
> 1. An **In-Ride Companion** for Careem, InDrive, and Uber drivers with live GPS hazard warnings.  
> 2. A **Command Console** for Motorway Police (NHMP) and Rescue 1122 control rooms with geohash cluster maps.  
> 3. A **Night HUD Cockpit** with high contrast and zero glare.  
> 4. And a **Public Corridor Risk Monitor** across 5 major Punjab routes."*
